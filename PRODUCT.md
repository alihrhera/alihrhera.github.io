# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, equally weighted: hiring managers and recruiters looking for senior or lead Android/mobile engineers, and freelance clients (business owners) hiring for Laravel web or Android work. Both arrive to judge credibility quickly and then make contact.

## Product Purpose
A personal CV and portfolio site for Ali Hrhera, Senior Software Engineer. It presents experience, selected work (Android apps, open-source PRs, freelance web platforms, a hardware project), certifications and contact routes. Success is a visitor reaching out by email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning
Android specialist with 8+ years of offline-first, high-performance mobile systems and on-device AI / NPU inference, who also ships backend and full-stack work and mentors other engineers (founder of VADT).

## Operating Context
Visitors skim the portfolio, filter by category (Android, Open Source, Community, Full Stack) and follow links to live sites and GitHub pull requests. Content is bilingual.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step.
- Arabic and English, with full RTL support, switchable at runtime; copy lives in `assets/main.js`.
- Dark theme is the default; a light theme is available via a toggle and persists in localStorage.
- Portfolio cards are filterable and can mark private codebases.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Ali Hrhera" with the `<Ali />` wordmark and ATH monogram. Contact: alihrhera1@gmail.com, GitHub alihrhera, LinkedIn alihrhera.

## Evidence on Hand
Only claims already on the site may be used (for example 500k+ field units, 150+ engineers trained, merged open-source PRs, live freelance sites). Do not fabricate metrics, testimonials or clients.

## Product Principles
- Proof over adjectives: every claim points to a project, PR or live site.
- One site, two audiences: employers and clients must both find their answer quickly.
- Bilingual parity: no feature or layout that works in English only.
- Keep it static and lightweight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
RTL correctness and readable contrast in both themes are required; no other standard confirmed.
